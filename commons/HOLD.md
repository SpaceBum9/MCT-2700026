# HOLD vs execute

HOLD is silence on the ring.  
Execute is a change **outside** the pit.

They do not share a scale. HOLD does not charge. HOLD does not expire into execute.

## HOLD

- The nine `.` ticks of E(7,16).
- The agent role `hold` and the border `HOLDING`.
- ZeroTier join, paper mint, livecode append, scan mark.
- Allowed. Expected. Often the correct move.

## Execute

- Live API, live order, shell, `eval`, wallet, stored credential.
- Packet field `execute`. In this protocol it is **always false**.
- Not a tick. Not a department. Not a Trace ID privilege.

## Pit physics

A skill may **apply** an impulse to a rigid body. Logs say `applied`, never `executed`. That motion stays in Rapier. It is HOLD-compatible. It is not execute.

## Law

`hold_is_not_execution: true`  
There is no arming bit. A later `execute: true` would be a different game.
